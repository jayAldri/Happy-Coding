ANNOUNCEMENT TO ALL GRADUATING STUDENTS (4TH YEAR BSIT AND BSOA, 2ND YEAR COA,CCS,AHRT,AAIS,AHRD)

All prospective graduating students for the Second Semester Academic Year 2025-2026 are hereby advised to file their Application for Graduation at the Registrar’s Office.

Application Period: March 9-14, 2026 Venue: PTC Office of the Registrar Office Hours: 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM

Please bring the following requirements:
1. Completed Application for Graduation Form (PTC-OCR-F0005-REV 2025)
https://drive.google.com/
2. Updated Student Program Curriculum
3. Clearance Form (PTC_OCR-F0001-REV 2025)
https://drive.google.com/
4. Original BirthCertificate (PSA Issued)
5. Other Documents (Please check if submitted and provide photocopy)
- Form 137 form SHS
- TOR- Copy for PTC(If transferee)
Kindly ensure that you have completed all academic requirements and settled all financial obligations before filing your application.

Failure to apply within the given period may result in exclusion from the official list of candidates for graduation

For inquiries, please visit the Office of the Registrar.

Please be guided accordingly
- OFFICE OF THE COLLEGE REGISTRAR
